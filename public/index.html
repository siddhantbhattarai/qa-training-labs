How a training session works

1

Read the guidance

Pick a module in the Curriculum — it tells you what to test and which difficulty level to use.

2

Start Training → log in

Click Start Training, sign in with a demo account, and you're inside the practice app.

3

Test the app

Exercise Products, Cart and Orders. Try edge cases, invalid input, and unexpected workflows.

4

Report defects

Log what you find in the Bug Hunt and write it up with the Reporting Guide.

5

Compare to Stable

Switch to the Stable baseline to confirm the expected behaviour, then diff the buggy levels against it.
